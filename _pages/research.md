## Working Papers

**The Costs of Decreased Financial Statement Comparability: Evidence from Divergent Leasing Standards**  
with Jeremy Michels and Rachel Zhang  
*Accepted for the 2026 Review of Accounting Studies Conference*

*Exploiting a sudden decrease in EBITDA comparability brought on by contemporaneous rule changes to leasing under IFRS and US GAAP, we document that reduced comparability across regimes increases information processing costs for analysts.*

---

**When Credit Unions Acquire Community Banks**  
with Laurel Mazur

*I examine how the rising trend of credit union acquisitions of community banks affects mortgage lending in low- and moderate-income communities.*

---

**Does the Credit Union Tax Exemption Distort Competition in Local Lending Markets?**  
with Jennifer Blouin

*We find that the credit union tax exemption distorts local mortgage market competition by exploiting the TCJA corporate tax cut as a shock to the relative tax advantage.*

---

**Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Expands Firm Disclosure but Rarely Changes Behavior**  
with Shawn Kim and Panos Patatoukas

*We examine the consequences of firms that adopt SASB standards and find limited capital market effects associated with adoption.*

---

**The Sound of Whose Voice? Comment Letter Influence at the FASB**  
with Rose Hartnett and Cassie Mongold

*We examine how stakeholder advocacy in FASB comment letters is associated with changes in FASB board decision-making in the final standards.*

---

**Opening the Black Box of CECL: Quantitative Models and Qualitative Adjustments**  
with Brad Badertscher and Laurel Mazur

*Despite regulatory concerns that smaller financial institutions do not produce informative qualitative adjustments under CECL, we document that small credit unions' qualitative adjustments are informative about future charge-offs, with predictive content driven primarily by soft local information rather than observable macroeconomic forecasts.*

---

**The Calibration of Cost Claims in Accounting Standard Setting**  
with Cassie Mongold

*We examine whether stakeholders' representation of adoption costs in FASB comment letters are consistent with actual observable adoption costs.*

---

**Local Deposit Competition and Depositor Discipline: Evidence from Credit Union Expansions**  
with Rui Shi and Detian Yang

*Exploiting a setting where banks faced a sudden increase in local deposit competition from credit unions, we find that expanding depositor choice sets strengthens depositor discipline, making bank funding flows significantly more sensitive to both financial performance and service quality.*

## Work in Progress

**Competition and Bank Risk-Taking**  
Solo-authored

*Exploiting a setting where community banks faced a sudden increase in competition from credit unions, I find that banks respond by taking more risk while engaging in earnings management to mask deteriorating performance.*
